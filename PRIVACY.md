# Timeout Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 21, 2026

Timeout is designed to work locally on your Android device. It does not require an account and the application does not request Internet access.

## Information Stored On Your Device

Timeout stores the information needed to provide its features, including:

- Apps you select
- Session and routine settings
- Draft setup information
- Local action history
- Appearance and protection preferences

This information remains in the app's local storage. Timeout does not operate a server that receives it.

## Accessibility Service

Timeout uses an Android AccessibilityService to detect app and window package transitions so it can show the blocking screen for selected apps.

The service:

- Does not retrieve screen text or message content
- Does not inspect the accessibility view hierarchy
- Does not capture screenshots
- Does not transmit accessibility data

## Data Control

You can delete local plans and history from Timeout Settings after any active Locked commitments have ended. Android's system settings also control app data, accessibility access, and uninstallation.

## Third Parties

Timeout does not include advertising or analytics services. Android and your device manufacturer may process system-level information under their own privacy policies when you install the app or change permissions.

## Changes

Material changes to this policy will be published in this repository and reflected in a future app release.
